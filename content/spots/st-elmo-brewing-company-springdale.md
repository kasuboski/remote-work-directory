St. Elmo's Springdale location brings the same craft beer expertise to East Austin. This modern brewery offers a spacious taproom with plenty of seating for remote work, featuring their signature beers alongside seasonal releases. The industrial-chic space provides a different vibe from the South location while maintaining the same community focus and dog-friendly policy.
