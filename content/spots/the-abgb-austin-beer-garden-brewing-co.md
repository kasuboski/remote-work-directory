Award-winning craft brewery named Brewpub of the Year at the Great American Beer Festival three consecutive years (2016-2018). Features dog-friendly beer garden, artisanal pizza, sandwiches, and salads with live music at least 3 days per week. Philosophy of 'Beer Makes It Better' creates a welcoming atmosphere for remote workers seeking quality food, craft beer, and community connection in South Austin.

## Food Notes
Pizza, sandwiches, salads (11:30am–10pm)
