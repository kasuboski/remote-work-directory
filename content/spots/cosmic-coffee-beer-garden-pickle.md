A unique permaculture-inspired coffee shop and beer garden in South Austin featuring specialty coffee, locally brewed beer, and craft cocktails in a stunning garden setting. Built on principles of sustainability with rainwater harvesting, chicken coops, composting, and raised-bed gardens. Nationally recognized wildlife habitat with food trucks and live music. Perfect for remote work in a natural, eco-friendly environment.
