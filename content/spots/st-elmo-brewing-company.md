A relaxed, unpretentious brewery in South Austin's St. Elmo neighborhood featuring a bar, beer hall, and beer garden. Popular for daytime laptop work with spacious seating and dog-friendly atmosphere. Hosts live music, yoga events, and community pint nights. On-site food trucks serve fried chicken and smash burgers. Perfect for remote workers who enjoy craft beer in a laid-back, community-oriented setting.
